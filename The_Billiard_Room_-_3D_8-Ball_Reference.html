Official 8-Ball Rules The table starts Open on the break. The first player to legally pocket a Solid (1–7) or Stripe (9–15) claims that group. Once all 7 balls of your group are cleared, legally pocket the 8-Ball to win. Hitting the wrong group first, failing to contact any ball, or scratching grants the opponent Ball-in-Hand.
3D Jump Shots (Negative Butt Elevation) Slide Butt Elevation into negative degrees (-25° to -4°) to angle the cue downward into the upper hemisphere of the cue ball. A blue 3D Parabolic Jump Arc previews where the airborne cue ball will hop and land.
Camera Perspectives & Overhead 360° Aiming Switch between Cue View, Orbit View, and Top View (or press V). In Top View, the overhead lamp automatically hides and you can drag in a 360° circle around the cue ball—or tap any ball/pocket—to aim directly at it.
Keyboard & Fine-Aim Shortcuts Hold Shift while dragging (or use ←/→ & A/D) for micro-angle adjustments. Hold and release Spacebar to charge and strike. Press Esc for this menu.
